When to Choose PostgreSQL
Complex relations requiring multi-table JOINs and foreign key guarantees.
Financial ledgers, transactional ordering, and mission-critical audit trails.
Advanced geospatial (PostGIS) and full-text vector embeddings (pgvector).
When to Choose MongoDB
Polymorphic catalogs, content management, and rapidly shifting schemas.
High-throughput write workloads and massive scale across distributed clusters.
Natural object-mapping for JavaScript / TypeScript microservices.
Verdict
PostgreSQL is the gold standard default for structured relational domains needing uncompromising consistency. MongoDB excels when horizontal distributed sharding and unstructured hierarchical documents are core to domain agility.
